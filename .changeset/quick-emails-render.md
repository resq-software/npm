Add @resq-sw/email-templates: typed transactional email templates (Effect Schema contract, React Email in the dark-first ResQ brand), a themeable renderEmail with per-render overrides, a createMailer factory for composing custom template suites, otp/welcome/password-reset/notification/incident-alert templates, and an optional Resend sender.
